# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want research disagreements to be precise, constructive, and welcoming. Contributors should:

- critique claims and methods rather than people;
- distinguish verified facts, paper claims, and personal interpretation;
- disclose relevant affiliations or conflicts when they materially affect a contribution;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption;
- respect paper authors, tool maintainers, and other contributors even when correcting their work.

## Research-integrity expectations

Do not inflate results, hide comparison caveats, fabricate citations, or present model-only experiments as product-agent evidence. Good-faith corrections are encouraged and will be recorded transparently.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository Security tab. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
